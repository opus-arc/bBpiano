9. Interpretation
[DYNAMICS]
L1 mean T60 low/high=0.340/0.270 s; v2a=0.343/0.032 s. Compare bands above and alpha/T60 plots for frequency-dependent damping.
Spacing CV L1/v2a=0.358/0.364; modal density is reported by band above; delay bank is identical.
[READOUT]
B and C have identical feedback matrices and poles. Their different output operators give different mode residues/zeros, hence different impulse and audible spectra.
Level-matched high-band C-B 20-50 ms: +18.61 dB
Level-matched high-band C-B 50-100 ms: +7.54 dB
Level-matched high-band C-B 20-200 ms: +18.61 dB
At matched 2 s RMS, 20-50 ms high-band C is between L1 and B: L1=0.00333251, B=6.54936e-05, C=0.000557854.
The same ordering does not apply to every metric: level-matched C-B 20-200 ms broadband=-1.47 dB, mid-band=-1.63 dB. C does not return to L1 feedback dynamics.
